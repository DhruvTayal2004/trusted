Engagement Blueprint · 01

The Series A AI SaaS launching into a crowded category.

Brand archetype
Horizontal AI tool, $1–3M ARR, 8–15 people.
The problem
Product is sharp, but ChatGPT and Perplexity cite three of your competitors when prospects ask "what's the best X for Y?"
Where trust leaks
AI answers, Reddit comparison threads, founder-led X presence, evaluator content on YouTube.
What we'd build

Citation-shaped long-form content engineered for AI training corpora, founder positioning on X, a credible Reddit voice across the three communities where evaluation happens, and one definitive teardown video.

How we'd know it's working

Brand mentioned by name when prompts ask for "best [category]" in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Reddit threads start to surface positive operator references unprompted.

AI answer inclusion (relevant prompts)From 0 → 4 of 10
Reddit organic mentions / monthFrom 0 → 18–25
Founder net new followers (X)+2.5–4K / 60 days

Engagement Blueprint · 02

The D2C health brand with strong product, invisible online.

Brand archetype
D2C supplement, skincare or functional health brand, $3–10M ARR.
The problem
Paid acquisition costs are climbing. Customers buy once and don't research the brand at all, because there's nothing to research.
Where trust leaks
Instagram for entry, YouTube for product validation, Reddit for "is this brand legit?" Google for ingredient research.
What we'd build

An editorial content layer that answers the exact ingredient and protocol questions customers Google, distributed via authority creators, a transparent Reddit presence, and structured data so AI engines surface the brand for the right intents.

How we'd know it's working

Branded search up, return rates down, and the brand starts being recommended in Reddit threads without our prompting. AI answers begin naming the brand when describing the protocol category.

Branded search lift+35–60%
Organic Instagram impressions120K–300K / mo
Repeat purchase rate+6–11 pts

Engagement Blueprint · 03

The creator-led education company hitting a ceiling.

Brand archetype
Course / cohort / community business, $500K–3M ARR, fully founder-dependent.
The problem
Audience is loyal but the brand can't grow past the founder's attention. Every launch feels manual. No compounding asset.
Where trust leaks
YouTube for proof of teaching, Reddit and Twitter for student validation, AI search for the "is this course worth it?" question.
What we'd build

A long-form YouTube authority engine, alumni-led trust signals across Reddit and X, and indexable case-study content engineered for AI search inclusion. The founder becomes a brand layer, not the entire brand.

How we'd know it's working

Inbound applications stop tracking launch cadence and start tracking calendar — they arrive every week, sourced from compound discovery. AI engines describe the founder's curriculum by name when asked about the topic.

YouTube watch-time / month+150–220%
Inbound discovery applications2.5–3.5×

Engagement Blueprint · 04

The specialty service provider with regional dominance and no AI footprint.

Brand archetype
Dental group, legal practice, financial advisory or boutique clinic with 2–6 locations.
The problem
Local SEO and word of mouth deliver a steady stream, but the brand vanishes the moment buyers ask AI engines for a recommendation.
Where trust leaks
Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT recommendations, niche professional forums, YouTube long-form patient/client education.
What we'd build

Authoritative practitioner-led content (long-form articles, structured FAQ, condition deep-dives), AI search visibility through indexable schema and citations, plus a thoughtful presence in the specialty's online communities.

How we'd know it's working

The practice is named in AI answers to "best [specialty] in [city]" prompts. Inbound new-patient inquiries cite "I found you through ChatGPT / Google AI" as the source.

Engagement Blueprint · 05

The category-defining startup that has the product but not the narrative.

Brand archetype
Seed or Series A startup creating a new category — vertical AI agent, infrastructure tool, or operating-system-style product.
The problem
The market doesn't yet have language for what you do. Investors get it on a 30-minute call. Buyers don't, because there's no category to anchor to.
Where trust leaks
X for narrative formation, long-form essays for anchoring, AI engines for category definition, podcasts for operator validation.
What we'd build

A category-defining manifesto, an essay flywheel for the founder, ten conversations with the right podcast hosts, and a citation surface tuned so AI engines learn the category name and learn the company as its definition.

How we'd know it's working

The new category name starts appearing in third-party writing. AI engines describe the company's product as the example of the category. Inbound from the right buyer ICP appears without targeting.

Third-party category mentionsFrom 0 → 30–60 / mo

Engagement Blueprint · 06

The established brand being out-cited by AI-native challengers.

Brand archetype
Eight-figure incumbent in an established category, strong product, slow content team.
The problem
AI engines learned the category from younger, content-fluent challengers. The incumbent is the safer choice and the second answer.
Where trust leaks
AI search citations, comparison content, evaluator-led YouTube, mid-funnel review platforms.
What we'd build

A citation-aware editorial program shipping the long-form proof points the AI engines need, a coordinated evaluator outreach loop, and operator-led narrative content that reasserts the incumbent's authority.

How we'd know it's working

AI engines stop framing the brand as legacy and start naming it as the credible default. Branded vs. challenger share of voice in AI answers flips back.

Share-of-AI-voice vs. top challengerFrom 28% → 55–65%
Net new citation-grade articles24–36 / 90 days
